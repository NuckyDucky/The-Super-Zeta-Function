What moves through these planes?

Each start sets a complex value of H(w,b) = ζ(w,b) + ζ(w,1−b). The point w moves so that H keeps that value as b increases from about 0.24116208 to ¼. Only the marked zero path has the requirement H = 0. The plots display the starting and finishing positions of the sampled paths.

The local scale is the final distance divided by the initial distance for two infinitesimally close starts. Values below one bring neighboring landings closer. Values above one spread them apart. The coordinates are numerical approximations. A, B, and the 37 refined centers display the midpoints of their interval checks.
