Multiplier coordinates near the calibrated period-640 Dottie flank

Write L=η+iy with η=4.749479070945369×10^(-8), and let K be the compact
subsegment on which the continuously marked orbit turns from 60° to 45°.
G(L,p)=(z ↦ exp(L exp(−Lz)))^320(p) denotes the 320-fold scalar iterate.
The glued trapping-disk certificate selects p with G(L,p)=p and
μ(L)=∂p G(L,p). Its continued rooted ordered pair has period 640.

Proposition. There is an open neighborhood U of K on which the selected
multiplier μ(L) has a single holomorphic inverse on μ(U). On K its
modulus is strictly decreasing with the clockwise turn h (in degrees).

Proof. Each stage is entire in both variables. At a selected periodic
point, ∂p(G−p)=μ−1 is nonzero by the strict contraction. The complex
implicit function theorem extends p, and hence μ, holomorphically to a
disk about each L on K. These extensions agree along overlaps of the
segment by uniqueness of the continued marking. Taking disks centered on
the segment, the identity theorem glues them on their intersections.

Every stage derivative is nonzero since L≠0, and their lap product is μ.
Let θ=arg μ in radians on the calibrated branch. The directed derivative
dθ/dy>0 throughout K shows that μ'(L)≠0: the imaginary part of
iμ'(L)/μ(L) is dθ/dy. The inverse function theorem gives a conformal
chart near each L. The 15° range and strict angle monotonicity give
injectivity of μ on K. Suppose μ failed to be injective on every
shrinking tube around K. Take unequal L_n,L'_n in the 1/n tubes with
μ(L_n)=μ(L'_n). Subsequencing gives limits in K. They coincide by
injectivity there, contradicting local injectivity at their common limit.
This supplies U. There is no numerical claim for the tube width.

The orbit computation recovers the complex quantity m_y=∂y log μ.
The real part measures radial change; its imaginary part measures angle
change in radians. Thus, for h counting clockwise degrees,

  d log|μ| / dh = −(π/180) Re(m_y)/Im(m_y).

The 448-bit interval certificate contraction-sweep.json bounds this on
120 cells strictly between −0.008614 and −0.005387. The endpoint moduli
are 0.204296772848039024... and 0.183971328451083271... . In particular
|μ| alone distinguishes every value of h on this one continued segment.
The Cauchy–Riemann equations also give ∂x log|μ|=∂y arg μ here.

Connection to the Hurwitz shift. After shrinking to a symmetric thin
simply connected tube, let m be the branch of log μ with Im m=θ on K.
Reflection across the calibration line is L* = 2η − conj L. Then

  B(L) = 1/2 − [m(L) − conj(m(L*))]/(2πi)

is holomorphic there. On K it equals the prescribed shift
b=1/2−θ/π. Its nonzero derivative along K and strict monotonicity on K
again give univalence after shrinking the tube. Consequently the actual
real-parameter calibration has a unique holomorphic extension as a germ
along this flank. The expression A(B(L)) uses this extension when A is the
labeled Hurwitz summand along the analytically continued sixth branch.
At its two different angles with the same A, μ and |μ| remain distinct.
