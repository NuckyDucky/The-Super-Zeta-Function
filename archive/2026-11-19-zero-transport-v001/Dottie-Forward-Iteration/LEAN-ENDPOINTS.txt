Endpoint factorization, classification and the reset

The checked reference is exactly the function used in the continuation:
  R(b,w) = pi^(-w) [HurwitzZeta(w,b) + HurwitzZeta(w,1-b)].
It is DottieOriented.LiuConnection.reference in the published project.

ForwardCertificate.endpoint_partition proves that for every complex w other
than 1, R(1/4,w)=0 if and only if w=2*pi*i*k/log(2) for some integer k or
ordinary Riemann zeta is zero there. dyadic_both_anchors_nonriemann proves
that the lattice addresses are indeed zeros at BOTH anchors and that ordinary
Riemann zeta is nonzero there. This proves disjointness. In particular k=4
gives 8*pi*i/log(2). The lattice need not be sampled to obtain the result.

The argument combines the residue-class factorization with exp(u)=1 iff
u=2*pi*i*k. If Re(w)=0 and w is nonzero, a hypothetical zeta zero transfers
under the functional equation to 1-w, on the established zero-free line
Re=1. At the origin zeta(0)=-1/2. The archived ReferenceReset.endpoint_reenters
proves an endpoint can be reused at exactly the same w after the reset.
FlankReferences.flank_zero_order preserves Riemann zero multiplicities in
Re(w)>0 away from the pole at both endpoints.

These are universal exact theorems. Which root reaches which endpoint in the
recorded sweeps, and which ordinal that endpoint has, are separate numerical
certificates. No succession, target ordinate, or enclosure was supplied to
the endpoint theorems.
