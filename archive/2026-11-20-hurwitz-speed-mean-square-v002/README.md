# The classical two-input zeta kernel

In the opening comparison of my [two-input spectrum proof](../2026-11-20-hurwitz-speed-mean-square-v001/two-input-spectrum.md#which-two-things-enter-the-sum), I cited §2 of Hedenmalm, Lindqvist and Seip for the Hilbert space of Dirichlet series. Their **explicit formula for its kernel is in §4.3, pp. 16–17**. For $`\Re x,\Re y>1/2`$ they take the orthonormal basis $`n^{-x}`$ and obtain

~~~math
K(x,y)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-x}\overline{n^{-y}}=\zeta(x+\bar y).
~~~

This is the classical precedent for pairing equal indices before summation. In my calculation an index denotes a squared determinant root, with a complex argument as well as a radius. The argument produces the hyperbolic cosine in the two-shift formula.
