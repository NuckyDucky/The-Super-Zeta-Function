## Use the contour to read counts and locations

`CircleArgumentPrinciple.proof` takes a holomorphic $f$ on an open neighborhood of a closed disk, a positive radius, and nonvanishing on its boundary. For any analytic weight $\varphi$, it equates $(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint \varphi\,f'/f$ with $\sum_\rho m_\rho\varphi(\rho)$. The weights $1,s,s^2$ give the multiplicity count and the first two moment sums. A signed comparison gives the difference of those sums between two readings. It can inspect the two raw Dottie determinants in $v$, or the cross-product and its analytic weight in $s$, separately. The input to certify for either use is boundary nonvanishing of the functions whose logarithmic derivatives are integrated. [Weighted circle proof](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta/Heights/ComplexAnalyticSets/PCircleArgumentPrinciple.lean#L231-L242)

`exists_localZeroExpansion_of_bound` gives a second route on a disk of radius $r>0$. If $f(z_0)\ne0$ and $|f|\le M|f(z_0)|$ throughout that disk, the sum of the zero multiplicities in the concentric $r/3$ disk is at most $\log M/\log 2$. On $r/6$, the log derivative minus the listed zero poles has error at most $72\log M/r$, evaluated away from zeros. This tells me exactly which growth bound to prove if I want a uniform remainder for a local pole comparison. [Zero expansion and error bound](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/Formalism/LandauZeroFreeBounds/ExceptionalZeroBounds/ExistsLocalZeroExpansionOfBound.lean#L49-L59)

## Compare heights and Mellin windows in the same variable

`zerosWindow_closed` gives an absolute $C>0$ with at most $C\log(N(|t|+2))$ zeros counted with order in $|\Im w-t|\le1$ in the open critical strip, for a primitive Dirichlet character of modulus $N$. Thus the ordinary case and primitive character cases have a bound on the half-height window in the $s=w/2$ coordinates. `exists_good_height` selects a $T'$ in $[T,T+1]$ separated from all absolute zero ordinates by the stated reciprocal-log distance. Its conclusion chooses $T'$ inside the interval. [Proved window bound](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/Formalism/PeriodIntegrals/ExplicitFormulaCounts/ExplicitFormula.lean#L5-L10) · [Exact window type](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/Formalism/PeriodIntegrals/ZerosWindow.lean#L7-L16) · [Good height and bound](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/Formalism/PeriodIntegrals/UnitaryGroupMassFormula/ExplicitFormula/GoodHeight.lean#L161-L164)

`explicitFormula_closed` gives a truncated prime-power relation for that same primitive family and $2\le T\le u$. The summand at an ordinary input $w=\rho$ is $m_\rho u^\rho/\rho$, which becomes $m_\rho u^{2s}/(2s)$ on the spectral scale. The error depends on $N,u,T$ and the sum of multiplicity divided by absolute value for zeros with height below one as specified in the theorem. This is a way to compare a native spectral window with a proved arithmetic window while preserving the scale and multiplicity. [Explicit formula and error](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba2a065656ec7c638baa601994de3e0245ff6665/src/Formalism/PeriodIntegrals/ExplicitFormula.lean#L9-L26) · [Original reconstruction](https://github.com/NuckyDucky/The-Super-Zeta-Function/blob/eaa526828cf700a2f43aeeb1c81de4309be5afee/lean/SuperZeta/UnitSpectrum.lean#L34)

## Identify the quartic Gaussian Euler factors prime by prime

`artinL_eq_LFunction` identifies the Artin $L$-function of a one-dimensional Galois character with the corresponding Hecke $L$-function on $\Re w>1$. In the proof the Euler-factor comparison splits according to whether the prime divides the conductor. The quadratic factorization proof shows how to use the regular representation, the trivial character and the quadratic character together. I can use this template over $\mathbb Q(i)$ to compare the existing Gaussian factor with the one-dimensional character of the quadratic extension, including the ramified factors. It needs the exact field, character, and comparison with my Gaussian coefficients.
